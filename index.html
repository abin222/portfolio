01 / Foundation

About & Dual-Expertise Architecture

I operate at the intersection of rigorous academic research and production-ready software engineering. With a Master of Technology in Artificial Intelligence from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (advised by Dr. Dhanya M Dhanalakshmy) and a Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology from Viswajyothi College of Engineering and Technology, my foundation unites deep algorithmic theory with robust software architecture.

In industry, I have led technical teams and engineered resilient systems across the full stack. At Bethel Technologies, I served as Technical Team Lead and Software Developer, directing cross-functional teams to build e-commerce and enterprise mobile/web applications using Laravel, Vue, Flutter, and Agile Scrum. Prior to that, at EngimaX Solutions, I developed mission-critical ERP & CRM systems for pharmaceutical and transport sectors, and at Viswajyothi College, I architected in-house automation tools and managed the institutional ReactJS web ecosystem.

In academia, I currently serve as Assistant Professor in Computer Science at the College of Engineering Cherthala. I deliver curriculum lectures, oversee academic development, and mentor final-year engineering projects in AI, Blockchain, and data-driven systems. My research spans medical image segmentation using evolutionary hyperparameter optimization—published with IEEE—and evaluating privacy-preserving local RAG architectures with Mistral 7B.
